Educational AI Daily tells you every morning what was discussed the day before in three open teaching communities: ChatGPT-IA-edu, Vibe Coding Educativo and EduMedia-IAG. Tools, debates, guides and resources on artificial intelligence in education, with every fact checked against its original source.

It is not an official publication and is not linked to any public body or any artificial intelligence company.

EVERY DAY
• The day's news, by section: Tools, Vibe coding, Multimedia, Debates, Institutions & Industry, and Library.
• The comic strip of Chalk and Algorithm, who comment with humour on a story from the issue.
• "A year ago": what the groups were discussing on the same day last year, told from today.
• On Sundays, a feature on one topic of the week.
• The week and the month, with all their stories, and the archive with every issue.

NEW ISSUE ALERT, IF YOU WANT IT
From "New issue alerts", in the footer, the app tells you each morning when the new issue is out. It comes switched off: only those who want it turn it on.

OFFLINE
The latest issue loaded and the pages already opened can be read offline.

WRITTEN WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
It is made every night by artificial intelligence agents (Claude, by Anthropic): they choose the topics, write, check every fact against its source, draw and translate. How they work and what is checked, in "The newsroom": https://diarioiaeducativa.github.io/en/redaccion.html

PRIVACY
No accounts and no ads. The paper counts visits to each page without cookies and without keeping anything about its readers, and the app collects no data. Privacy policy: https://diarioiaeducativa.github.io/en/redaccion.html#privacidad

IN FIVE LANGUAGES AND OPEN
• In English, Spanish, Catalan, Galician and Basque.
